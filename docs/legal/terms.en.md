## 1. About these terms

Itqan is an online learning platform where teachers run their own academies. These terms apply to everyone who uses it: students, parents and guardians, teachers, assistants and administrators. The operator is [TO CONFIRM: legal name]. By creating an account or using the platform, you accept these terms and the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.en.md).

**Students under 18** may use Itqan only with the consent of a parent or guardian, given at registration. The parent or guardian accepts these terms on the student's behalf.

## 2. Your account

- One account belongs to one person. Give your real name and an email address you control.
- Keep your password private. You are responsible for what happens under your account.
- Teachers, assistants and administrators must turn on two-factor authentication (TOTP) before they can manage an academy.
- Tell the academy staff at once if you think someone else has used your account.

## 3. Devices and sign-in

To protect paid courses:
- Each student account can be used on a limited number of devices, set by the teacher.
- Only one device can be signed in at a time. Signing in somewhere else while a session is open is blocked; you can ask staff to sign out the other device.
- To use a new device after reaching the limit, send a device-change request from that device; staff approve or reject it.
- Sharing your account, password or devices with other people is not allowed.

## 4. Courses, access and payment

- Each academy's teacher decides its courses, prices and who may join.
- You get access through an email invitation, a single-use access code, or by staff adding you. Access codes work once, for one course, and must not be sold on or shared.
- **Payment happens between you and the teacher, outside the platform.** Itqan does not take payments from students. [TO CONFIRM: whether the operator is a party to any payment, refund or dispute between students and teachers.]
- A teacher can end a student's access to a course. Your past grades stay in your record.

## 5. Course content

- Course content (videos, files, exams and texts) belongs to the teacher who published it, or to whoever licensed it to them.
- Students get a personal right to view the content of courses they have access to, while they have access. You may not copy, record, download (unless the teacher allows a download), share, sell or publish it.
- Videos show a watermark with your name and email address. A recording that is shared can be traced to the account it came from.
- Teachers must have the right to publish everything they upload, and must not upload unlawful content.

## 6. Exams

- Do your own work. Using someone else's help, another account, or tools the teacher has not allowed is cheating.
- An exam attempt is tied to the device you started it on. Staff may extend time or move an attempt to another device in special cases.
- Grades and answer reviews are released at the times the teacher sets.

## 7. Acceptable use

You must not:
- try to reach data, accounts or academies that are not yours;
- test, probe or overload the platform, or get around its security;
- post insulting, unlawful or harmful content in lesson questions or support requests;
- impersonate another person;
- use the platform in a way that breaks Saudi law.

## 8. Teachers and academy staff

Teachers and their assistants:
- manage their students' access and data only for running their courses;
- keep their students' data confidential and follow the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.en.md);
- are responsible for the content they publish and for their dealings and payments with their students.

## 9. Suspension and closing accounts

- Academy staff can suspend a student's access to their academy. Platform administrators can suspend any account or pause an academy, for example after a breach of these terms or a security risk.
- You can ask to close your account and delete your data as described in the privacy policy.
- When an academy is closed, its data and files are deleted 30 days later.

- We may change the platform and these terms. We announce important changes inside the platform before they apply.
